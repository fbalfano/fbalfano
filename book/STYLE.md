# House Style - Writing Rules

These rules apply to ALL prose in this book: chapters, hooks, TOC, indexes.
Check this file before drafting anything new.

## Punctuation

1. **No em-dashes (—), ever.** Replace with a comma, hyphen, or semicolon,
   whichever fits the sentence. Restructuring the sentence (e.g., splitting it
   in two) is also fine. En-dashes in ranges (paras. 15–39) are allowed.
2. Exception: text inside a **verbatim quotation** from a court decision keeps
   the original punctuation exactly as the court wrote it.

## Chapter structure

3. Every chapter opens with a **Hook Sentence in bold** as the first line after
   the title. Short, punchy, spoiler-flavored.
4. Chapter titles are punchy and playful where the case allows it (e.g., "Don't
   Murder a Woman Wearing a Fitbit", "Never Rent a Getaway Car").
5. Every chapter is a **standalone short story**; no chapter depends on another.
   Cross-references are optional "see also" pointers only.
6. Every chapter ends with a "How'd they get caught?" payoff section followed by
   a **Read the case yourself** call-out: full citation, court, date, free link.

## Accuracy

7. Facts come from the anchor decision. Anything sourced from news coverage is
   flagged in the text as news-sourced.
8. Citations are confirmed against the slip opinion PDF in `book/pdfs/` before a
   chapter is marked drafted.
