This is a work of nonfiction. Every case in this book is real. Each chapter is built
on a published appellate court decision, and the facts are drawn from those
decisions and, where noted, from the surrounding public record. The narrative
reconstruction, the pacing, and the commentary are the author's; the underlying
facts belong to the courts. Where a chapter draws on material outside the anchoring
decision, that is flagged in the text. Where a court ordered that a victim's or
witness's identity be protected, this book keeps that person to initials.

Nothing in this book is legal advice, and nothing in it should be read as a
statement about the guilt or innocence of any living person beyond what the cited
courts themselves concluded. Several of these cases were later reopened, reversed,
or retried; the book says so, in plain terms, wherever it happened.
