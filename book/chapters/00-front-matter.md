# HOW NOT TO GET AWAY WITH MURDER

### True stories of the evidence that caught them, anchored to the court decisions that prove it

*Working manuscript*

---

Every chapter in this book is built on a real, published appellate court
decision: from the Supreme Court of Canada, the provincial Courts of Appeal,
the United States Supreme Court, and the American state high courts. The
storytelling is ours; the facts are the courts'. At the end of every chapter,
a citation and a free link let you read the decision yourself.
