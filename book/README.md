# How Not to Get Away With Murder

*A true-crime book about the forensic threads that unravelled killers — each chapter anchored to a real Canadian appellate decision you can read for yourself.*

---

## The concept

Every chapter reverse-engineers a murder conviction through the single piece of
evidence that undid the killer: the residue on a hand, the ping off a cell
tower, the search typed into a hidden hard drive, the confession whispered to a
fake crime boss. We tell it as a story — *how did they get caught?* — and then
hand the reader the actual court decision so they can go read the facts
themselves.

**Jurisdiction:** Canada. Every anchor case is a decision of a provincial Court
of Appeal or the Supreme Court of Canada, available (free) on
[CanLII](https://www.canlii.org).

**Tone:** Popular true-crime. Narrative first, forensic science explained
accessibly, legal accuracy underneath.

## The rules we hold ourselves to

1. **Every case is real.** No invented citations, no invented facts. Where a
   case is contested, reversed, or thematically complicated, we say so and use
   it *as* that more interesting story.
2. **Every link resolves to the judgment.** See the verification note below.
3. **The court decision is the spine.** We dramatize the human story, but the
   factual matrix comes from what the appellate court actually found.

## ⚠️ Verification status — READ BEFORE PUBLISHING

The research for this table of contents was done in an environment where
**canlii.org is blocked at the network level**, so the CanLII pages could not be
opened directly. Every citation was instead cross-confirmed through independent
web sources (news reporting, law-firm case summaries, CanLII Connects, court
dockets, and CanLII URLs that surfaced in the search index).

That is strong evidence each case is real, but the CanLII links themselves
should be **clicked once from an unrestricted network before anything goes to
print.** In `case-index.md`, each case carries a status:

- ✅ **Verified** — citation and/or exact CanLII URL surfaced in the search
  index and was corroborated by multiple sources.
- ⚠️ **Confirm link** — citation confirmed by independent sources; CanLII URL
  built from the standard neutral-citation pattern. Click to confirm.
- 🔶 **Frame with care** — a real case, but contested, reversed, inverted, or
  otherwise not a clean "solved murder." These often make the *best* chapters,
  but must be told honestly.

## Files

- `TABLE-OF-CONTENTS.md` — the book's structure: parts, chapters, anchor cases,
  and the one-line "how they got caught" hook for each.
- `case-index.md` — the master list of every anchor case: style of cause,
  citation, CanLII link, court, year, verification status, and a factual
  summary of the smoking gun.
- `gaps-and-future-chapters.md` — forensic categories that don't yet have a
  Canadian appellate decision (genetic genealogy, Fitbit/wearables, smart-home
  IoT, licence-plate readers) and how to handle them.
