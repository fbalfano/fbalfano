About

The guy behind the name

Bad Ass AI was started by Frank Alfano, an entrepreneur who got tired of watching good businesses freeze up in front of AI.

Frank isn't an academic and doesn't play one on the internet. He's an operator — someone who builds and runs real businesses, and who has spent the last few years wiring AI into every one of them: automating the grunt work, speeding up the sales pipeline, and shipping things a team twice the size used to handle.

That's the whole premise of Bad Ass AI: the people best positioned to teach business AI are the ones actually running businesses with it. Not vendors selling software. Not consultants selling decks. Operators showing operators what works, what doesn't, and what's a waste of your money.

Whether it's done-for-you agency work, hands-on coaching, or straight-talk consulting, the goal never changes — make you and your team dangerous with AI, and plug you into a community so you're never figuring it out alone.
